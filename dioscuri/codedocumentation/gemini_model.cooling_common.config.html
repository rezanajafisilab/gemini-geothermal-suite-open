6.1.7.1. gemini_model.cooling_common.config

Explicit screening parameters (sources A002/A003/A006/A007/A013).

All temperature envelopes below are analyst screening domains, not supplier ratings. Capacities/flows default to zero: equipment must be explicitly installed/supplied. Ratios are W per W of the named duty; water cp is independent of Rules brine cp. See 03_COMPONENT_EQUATIONS and the parameter handoff for bounds/provenance.
