6.1.7.2. gemini_model.cooling_common.kernel

Shared vectorised cooling calculation, SI units; equations in specification 03.

Absorption-first is an explicit component policy under supplied boundaries. This does not schedule wells or allocate heating. Constant-COP envelopes are analyst scenarios. Chilled/CW pump heat feedback is solved algebraically for linear auxiliary proxies; hot-pump and fan heat goes to ambient. No iterations, caches, I/O or model calls.
