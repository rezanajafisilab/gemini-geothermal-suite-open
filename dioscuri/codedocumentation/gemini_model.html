6.1. gemini_model

GEMINI modeling package.

Provides abstract model interfaces and domain-specific subpackages for well hydraulics, pumps, fluids, reservoirs, and corrosion.

Modules

absorption_chiller

Single-effect absorption with installed electric backup.

adder

Adder models.

boiler

Boiler models.

booster_pump

Booster pump models.

chp

Combined heat and power (CHP) models.

compressor

Compressor models.

cooling_common

Pure shared cooling physics; no challenge-plant dependencies.

cooling_tower

Minimum tower wrapper with thermal/water limits.

corrosion

CO2 corrosion modeling package.

electric_chiller

Water- and air-cooled electric screening component.

erosion

Erosion modeling package.

filter

Filter models.

fluid

Fluid PVT models.

heat_exchanger

Heat exchanger models.

heatpump

Heatpump models.

injector_pump

Injector pump models.

mixer

Mixer models.

model_abstract

Abstract base interfaces for Gemini simulation models.

pump

Pump models.

reservoir

Reservoir models.

separator

Separator models.

splitter

Splitter models.

well

Well hydraulics models.
