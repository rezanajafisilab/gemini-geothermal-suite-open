## Recommended values for the model

**Topic 1 – E-boiler and gas**
- E-boiler capex: 314 €/kW_th (PBL 259 + 55), plus 92 €/kW if the grid connection must be upgraded (total 407 €/kW, PBL 2026). Low case 70–155 €/kW (DEA, Danish, includes connection fees). No fixed part.
- Fixed O&M: 7.8 €/kW/yr maintenance plus grid capacity cost. Use 183 €/kW/yr (firm) or 91 €/kW/yr (non-firm ATR), per PBL forward-looking values. Today's Liander TS/MS tariff is about 123 €/kW/yr firm. For a backup unit, the Liander 600-h contract is about 24 €/kW/yr plus 2.16 €/kW per week used.
- Variable O&M: 0.84 €/MWh_th (DEA), plus electricity at the Eurostat price ÷ 0.99, plus energy tax.
- Efficiency 0.99; minimum load 5 %; cold start ≤5 min; full ramp in 30 s; forced outage 1 %; technical life 20–25 yr (15 yr economic); unit size 5–60 MW.
- Grid connection lead time and availability: treat as a hard constraint (congestion). Offer non-firm/ATR as an option, with no guarantee of availability.
- Produced gas with no gas boiler: the gas must still be burned (small gas-engine CHP or burner). Venting is prohibited. Flaring is only allowed for upsets or start-up (Mijnbouwbesluit art. 38). Count its CO2 against the Wcw 25 kg/GJ norm (from 2030).

**Topic 2 – Investor case**
- SDE++ basisbedrag: choose the category from geothermal capacity and full-load hours. With HP top-up to 100 °C, use "HT network with heat pump" (≥12 MWth basislast 0.1325 €/kWh, 6,000 h). Without HP, use 12–20 MWth basislast (0.0696, 6,000 h), middenlast (0.0997, 5,000 h) or ≥12 MWth geen basislast (0.1647, 3,500 h).
- Correctiebedrag = 0.70 × TTF[LHV]: base 0.0196 €/kWh long-term (0.0273 for 2026), floor 0.0131. Add the ETS2 correction of 0.0114 €/kWh from 2028 for basislast categories. Duration 15 yr. Subsidised output ≤ capacity × category full-load hours (25 % backward banking).
- Cooling earns no SDE++ in the geothermal categories.
- Heat revenue: 40.97 €/GJ incl. VAT (121.9 €/MWh excl. VAT) as a retail ceiling; fixed charge 827.91 €/yr per household. Tariffs become cost-based only in Wcw phase 2.
- Vpb 25.8 % (19 % on the first €200k). EIA: none on SDE++-covered parts; 40 % on an e-boiler (code 260301) if it is outside SDE++.
- Discount rate: 7.3 % nominal / 5.2 % real after tax (PBL), with sensitivity 5.6–8.7 %.

**Topic 3 – Failure economics**
- ESP failure rate: base 0.5/yr (MTBF 2 yr, 2026 Dutch evidence of 1–2 yr). Range 0.2/yr (5-yr design life) to 1.0/yr.
- ESP swap cost: 0.25–0.5 M€ per event (2016 Dutch/Paris Basin figure of 0.19–0.30 M€ uplifted for inflation; vendor ceiling €0.5 M). This excludes lost heat.
- ESP outage: with a spare and a contracted unit, 1–2 weeks (2–3 days best case, 1 week observed). Without a spare: model 3–6 months (6-month equipment lead time in a German case).
- Gas boiler forced outage 1 % (λ ≈ 0.04–0.13/yr, MTTR 3–43 h). Large HP forced outage 0–5 % (central 0 %) plus 1 week/yr planned. Centrifugal electric chiller λ 0.05/yr, MTTR 15 h. Absorption chiller λ 0.17/yr, MTTR 12 h. Heat exchanger λ 0.012/yr, MTTR 10 h. E-boiler 1 %. Geothermal plant 2 % plus 2 weeks/yr planned.
- Heat not delivered: value it at the marginal cost of backup heat (gas boiler incl. CO2 at 137 €/t). If there is no backup, use at least the lost revenue (~122 €/MWh). Add Warmteregeling compensation (€35 + €20 per extra 4 h per connection) only for network-side outages, since production shortfalls are generally exempt. Run a penalty sensitivity up to 10,000 €/MWh.
