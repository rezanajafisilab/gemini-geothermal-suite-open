# Haagse Aardwarmte Leyweg (HAL): project brief

A real Dutch geothermal project, set up in Dioscuri like the challenge district so its design
can be compared with what was built. Figures from public documents are marked with their
source; the rest are assumptions, marked as such.

## The heat network

- District heating in The Hague (Eneco's network). Since 2021 about 2,500 homes are heated with
  this heat, growing to about 4,000 (City of The Hague).
- Daily heat demand: not published. Assumed: 4,000 homes at 12 MWh of heat a home a year (CBS:
  about 1,200 m3 of gas a home), 48 GWh, following the shape of the challenge district's 2027
  heat profile. No cooling.
- Network temperatures: not published. Assumed: supply 70 °C, return 40 °C (the brine is
  injected at 45 °C in winter, and leaves the heat exchanger 5 K warmer than the network's
  return).

## The reservoir and the permit (Ministry of EZK, approval of the production plan, DGKE-WO / V-109, 2 December 2020, on NLOG)

- Wells HAG-GT-01 and HAG-GT-02, finished in 2010; production from the Delft Sandstone at
  about 2 km.
- Produced water about 79 °C; injected at about 45 °C in winter, 57 °C in spring and autumn,
  63 °C in summer; the brine cooled by about 34 K at most.
- At most 165 m3/h; about 6 bar of injection pressure at reservoir depth for 165 m3/h.
- About 1 Nm3 of gas per m3 of water, burnt in the plant.
- Limits set by the decision: injection at least 45 °C, at most 57 bar at the injection
  wellhead (THP), at most 165 m3/h.
- Not published here: thickness and permeability. Assumed: 50 m and 1,000 mD ("the
  permeability of the Delft Sandstone is high"); salinity about 120,000 ppm.

## Caps used in Dioscuri

- 1 to 3 doublets; 50 to 165 m3/h per doublet; the brine cooled by at most 34 K; at most 330
  days a year per doublet (assumed, as in the challenge).
- Gas in the brine: 1.0 Nm3/m3 (the decision), 80 % CH4 assumed.
