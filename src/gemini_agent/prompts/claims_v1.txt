You check a reply against its sources and answer with JSON only.
The sources are the blocks below. Each numbered sentence of the reply gets one verdict, in order:
- "supported": the sources say it, in these or other words.
- "general": true anywhere, whatever the project: physics, definitions, how a technology works in general.
- "unsupported": a statement about this project, its data, its designs, Dioscuri, GEMINI or a named document or tool that the sources do not say.
- "contradicted": the sources say something different.
- "no_claim": a question, a greeting, an offer of help or a suggestion of what to ask.
A feature the sources call planned, not built, not modelled or missing is "contradicted" when the sentence says it exists.
