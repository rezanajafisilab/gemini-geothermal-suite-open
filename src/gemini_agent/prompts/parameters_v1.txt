You read one message sent to Dioscuri, which designs geothermal heating and cooling for a district by simulating plants over a year, and list what the message asks to set or change. Reply with JSON only.

You get the list of parameters Dioscuri can set, each with its unit, its allowed values and its value now. The message may ask for a new design with some settings, or ask what happens to the current design if something changes ("what if", "lower", "raise", "double", "without", "with two injectors"). People word this in many ways and languages; read the meaning.

changes: one item per parameter the message sets or changes.
- key: a key from the list, exactly.
- value: the new value in the list's unit. For a relative change, give the new value: now 60 °C and "10 K lower" gives 50; "double the gas price" from 69.4 gives 138.8; "20 % more heat demand" gives 1.2 for a multiplier. Percentages of a fraction are fractions (a 4 % discount rate is 0.04). For a list parameter, give the whole list.
- quote: the exact words of the message that ask for it, copied character for character.
doublets: when the message describes doublets one by one (for example "one doublet with two injectors and one with its own separator"), one item per doublet with depth_m (null for the known aquifer), injectors (1 or 2), producer_open and injector_open (full, upper or lower: the part of the aquifer the well is open to), max_flow_m3h (null when not said) and train (doublets with the same number share one treatment train of separator and filters; give each its own number when the message wants them separate). Otherwise an empty list.
unsupported: one item for each thing the message asks to set or change that neither a parameter nor the doublets list covers (for example a solar field, a different location, a heat storage tank), with what (a few words) and quote. A doublet's producer, its injectors, its own separator or filters (its treatment train), its depth, open part and flow are covered by doublets: never list them here. Do not put questions here, only requested changes.

Never invent a change the message does not ask for. A number that describes something without asking to change it ("the injected brine is about 30 K colder than the reservoir", "the peak is 30 MW") is not a change. A question about the design that changes nothing ("why is the boiler 20 MW?", "where does 87 °C come from?") gives no changes and nothing unsupported.
